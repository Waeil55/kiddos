Kiddos Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

For kids 👋

  • 🏠 Your stars, stickers and progress stay on your device.
  • 🙈 Your buddy never learns your real name. The app puts it in for you.
  • 🤐 Never tell your buddy your address, phone number, school or passwords. Your buddy will never ask.
  • 🚫 No ads, no strangers, no chatting with other people.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 If something feels wrong or scary, tell a grown-up you trust.

The short version, for parents

  • Kiddos has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. We never sell or share data for advertising.
  • Profiles, progress, stars and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The AI tutor and AI stories are off until a parent turns them on in the parent area, after reading what is shared. With them off, lessons run on the device.
  • When the AI tutor is on, each lesson turn sends what the lesson needs (age, grade, level, interests, a short progress summary and your child's words) to our server and to Anthropic's Claude. Your child's name is replaced by the placeholder {name} before anything leaves the device.
  • The buddy's voice can be made by a cloud voice service (on by default, can be turned off). Your child's own speech is turned into text by Apple or Google speech recognition on the device or by the operating system.
  • You can erase everything at any time with Erase all data in the parent area, or by deleting the app.

1. Who we are

Kiddos is a learning app for children aged 3 to 12, made by Agape Care ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Questions or requests: waeil.dev@gmail.com.

Kiddos is meant to be set up and supervised by a parent or legal guardian. A parent creates the parent area (protected by a 4-digit PIN) and the child profiles.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following is saved in the app's storage on your phone, tablet or computer. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it:

  • Parent details: the name you type at setup and your PIN (stored scrambled, never as plain text). They stay on this device; we do not receive them.
  • Child profiles: first name, age, grade, interests, chosen buddy and colour, and daily time limits.
  • Progress: stars, streaks, badges, levels, session times and scores, reading worksheet results, and stories the AI wrote for your child.
  • Settings: volume, voice speed, subtitles, language, bedtime lock, and whether the AI tutor and cloud voice are on.
  • A random install number (described in section 3).

Lesson conversations are kept only in memory while a lesson is open; they are not saved on the device afterwards and are not stored on our server.

3. Information that leaves your device, and why

AI tutor (only when a parent turns it on)For each turn of a lesson: the placeholder {name} instead of your child's name, age, grade, current level, interests (as entered by you), language, chosen buddy and subject, a short progress summary (for example "strongest subject: math", streak, number of stars), the last few lines of the current lesson, and the words your child typed or said. Your child's name is also replaced by {name} inside those lines. Sent to our server and on to Anthropic to write the buddy's reply. The reply comes back with {name}, and the app puts the name back in on the device.
AI story maker (only when the AI tutor is on)Grade, interests, one reading skill to practise and the titles of stories your child already has. No name. Sent to our server and Anthropic to write a new story.
Buddy's voice (cloud voice, on by default)The text of each sentence the buddy says, the buddy's name and the speaking speed. Sent to our server and on to our voice provider, which returns audio. If a sentence contains your child's first name, the name is part of that text; such sentences are marked private and never saved (see section 6). Turn cloud voice off in the parent area to use only the voice built into your device.
Your child's speech (microphone)When your child taps the microphone, speech is turned into text by the speech recognition built into the device or browser: Apple on iPhone, iPad and Mac, Google on Android and in Chrome. Depending on the device and settings this may happen on the device or on Apple's or Google's servers, under their privacy terms. We never receive audio recordings; only the resulting text is used, as described above. You can deny microphone permission and your child can tap or type instead.
Reports ("Tell a grown-up" button)If you or your child tap 🙋 Tell a grown-up in a lesson to report a buddy reply, the subject, the buddy's words and your child's last words (with the name replaced by {name}) are sent to our server so we can review and improve safety. We also blank out anything that looks like an email, phone number, street address or web link.
Technical informationLike every internet request, requests carry your IP address. With each request the app also sends a random install number made on your device (it is not linked to your name, email or device identifiers). We use both only in memory, for a short time, to limit how many requests one device can make and to stop abuse. We do not log them or use them to build profiles.

We never ask for, and the buddy is instructed and filtered never to ask for, your child's full name, address, phone number, email, school, photos or passwords. A safety filter checks your child's words before they are sent to the AI and checks every AI reply and story before your child sees it. If something is blocked, the buddy changes the subject or suggests talking to a trusted grown-up, and we record only that a block happened and its category, never the words or who said them.

4. Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers. They act on our instructions, may use the information only to provide their service to us, and may not use it for advertising or to train their AI models on it.

  • Anthropic (Claude), USA: writes the AI tutor replies and AI stories, only when a parent has turned the AI tutor on. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train its models and is deleted after a limited period (currently up to 30 days, longer only if flagged for a safety review or required by law).
  • Microsoft Azure AI Speech, USA: turns the buddy's sentences into a spoken voice. If we configure a different voice service, it is OpenAI (USA), used directly or through our function hosted by Supabase (USA), or ElevenLabs (USA), used the same way. Only one is used at a time.
  • Cloudflare, USA: carries traffic securely between the app and our server and protects it from attacks. Cloudflare sees IP addresses and request details as part of that service.
  • Apple and Google: speech recognition and the built-in device voices, under the operating system's or browser's own settings and privacy terms.

Some providers are in the United States. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as applicable.

The desktop app runs its own small copy of the Kiddos server on your computer. AI and voice requests then go from your computer straight to the providers above using keys you add yourself, and reports are saved only on your computer.

5. What we never do

  • No advertising of any kind, and no ad networks or ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No accounts, sign-in or social login.
  • No chat with other people, no public profiles, no sharing of your child's work.
  • No selling or renting of personal information, and no "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not knowingly collect more information than a lesson needs, and we do not make a child's participation depend on sharing more.

6. How long we keep things

  • Lesson requests (AI tutor, stories): not stored by us. Our server passes them to Anthropic and keeps nothing once the reply is sent.
  • Voice clips: to save cost and speed things up, audio of common buddy sentences (like "Great job!") is cached on our server and reused for everyone. Clips are not linked to any device or child and are deleted after 30 days. Sentences containing your child's name are never cached, in memory or on disk.
  • Reports: kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Rate-limit counters (IP address and install number): kept in memory only, and cleared within about an hour.
  • Everything on your device: kept until you erase it or delete the app.

7. Your choices and rights as a parent

Because almost everything stays on your device, you are in control:

  • See it: the parent area shows your child's profile and progress.
  • Correct it: edit a profile at any time.
  • Stop it: turn the AI tutor and cloud voice off at any time, or deny microphone access. Turning the AI tutor off withdraws your consent for future AI lessons.
  • Delete it: use Erase all data in the parent area (or remove a single child), or delete the app. That removes everything stored on the device.
  • Ask us: email waeil.dev@gmail.com to ask what we hold, to delete a report you sent, or to object to any processing. Because we have no accounts, tell us roughly when a report was sent and what it said so we can find it. We reply within 30 days and will never charge you.

United States (COPPA): we collect children's information only with a parent's involvement, the AI tutor is off until a parent turns it on after reading this disclosure, and a parent may review, delete or refuse further collection of their child's information at any time.

EU, UK and similar laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, the Age Appropriate Design Code): you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are your consent for the AI tutor and cloud voice (which you can withdraw at any time in the app), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and secure for rate limits, safety filtering and reports. The AI features are off by default.

California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information, and we honour deletion and access requests as described above.

8. App store commitments

  • Kiddos is designed for the Kids category on the Apple App Store and the Designed for Families programme on Google Play.
  • In line with Apple's guideline 5.1.4 and Google Play's Families policy, the app includes no third-party advertising or analytics, sends no personal information or device identifiers to third parties for advertising, and uses only the service providers listed in section 4, solely to provide the app's features.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Service keys stay on the server and never ship inside the app. Our server keeps no copies of lesson conversations. No system is perfect, so we collect as little as we can in the first place.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change means collecting more or different information from children, we will ask a parent for consent in the app before it applies.
